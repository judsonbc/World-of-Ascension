World of Ascension

Rise from mortal flesh to heavenly sovereign.

Every cultivator begins the same way: a mortal body, a worn blade and a gate that leads nowhere yet. Hunt the beasts beyond the hub, mine darksteel and temper your gear at the Lotus Forge, and learn a new skill every five levels as you break through realm after realm, from Mortal Body to Sovereign Tier 80 at level 1000.

Sixteen worlds wait behind the World Gate, each deadlier than the last. Bosses rise at random, so keep an eye on the minimap for red dots. Hunt alongside friends in shared worlds, or step into the Arena (World 0) where only the strongest survive. Loot is yours to keep, sell or equip, and the climb is yours alone to finish.
